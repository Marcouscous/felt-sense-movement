@ The Finnish Hall, Berkeley

A performance with no audience... everyone is in.

The Overscore is a collective improvisation event. Movement, voice, and live music weave into a shared narrative arc ritual. We begin by lying on the floor. From there we move through a series of scores (games, creative structures, spatial orientations) all strung together into one shared map. The container is held and carried throughout by live improvised music. For this iteration we have the very talented Ian Erickson creating the soundscape.
