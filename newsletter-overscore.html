The Overscore (dive straight in)

Live music by Ian Erickson

@ The Finnish Hall, Berkeley

Something new is taking shape.

The Overscore is a collective improvisation event. Movement, voice, and live music weave into a shared narrative arc ritual. We begin by lying on the floor. From there we move through a series of scores (games, creative structures, spatial orientations) all strung together into one shared map. The container is held and carried throughout by live improvised music. For this iteration we have the very talented Ian Erickson creating the soundscape.

FRIDAYS · 9:30–11:30am

The Finnish Hall · Berkeley

Kinetic Waves is a Friday morning movement class rooted in the application of the Axis Syllabus. We get curious about how our bodies actually work, then put that understanding to use through play, flow, and dancing. Whether you're brand new to moving or have years on the floor, there's room for you here. Come as you are.

Emily Jones × Marcus van Duren

Resonance & Response and beyond. Gratitude & looking forward to 2027.

I am grateful beyond words to have discovered a teaching collaborator who inspires me. The Axis Syllabus and Contact Improvisation are two practices we both love. But neither practice is necessarily the end in itself. Movement fluency, creative collaboration, and belonging are at the center of our work. In other words, helping people to come into a deeper relationship with themselves and each other.

In 2026, we taught four 3-day workshops in different cities. It's been a beautiful journey so far. Now we're looking forward to another season of workshops in 2027. Here are our pending dates, so you can save them on your calendar and stay in the loop:
