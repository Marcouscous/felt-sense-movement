Felt Sense Movement

Something new is taking shape.

The Overscore is a collective improvisation event — movement, voice, and live music woven into a shared ritual. There's no audience because everyone is in. You begin lying on the floor, let the day go, and gradually wake up to the people around you. From there we move through a series of scores — games, creative structures, spatial orientations — strung together into one shared map.

Live music by Ian Erickson throughout. Two sessions, two days. Come to one or both.

The Overscore (dive straight in)

The Finnish Hall · Berkeley

The Overscore is a long-form movement and voice improvisation structured by a series of scores — spatial games, creative limitations, and collective orientations that give shape to emergence. Saturday is a facilitated introduction to the structures. Sunday we arrive and dive in.

Rooted in the tradition of Nancy Stark Smith's Underscore, and influenced by contact improvisation, vocal improvisation, and improvisational performance.
